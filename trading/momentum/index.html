How this works

YTD VWAP estimates the average cost basis of shares traded this year. Above it, the average buyer is in profit; below it, the average buyer is underwater. Solid line is close, dashed line is VWAP, and triangles mark crosses. Sector, theme, and country charts show the same smoothed 50-session Z-score beneath price. Descriptive market data, not investment advice.
