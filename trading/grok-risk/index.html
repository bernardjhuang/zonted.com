Oil shock dominates: USO +6.74% while SPY slips -0.03% to 773.06 and QQQ -0.31% to 720.80; bonds sell (TLT -0.86%) and gold extends, cutting risk appetite from 7 to 4

US equities opened the week soft after Friday's broad Risk-on rally. In ETF terms: SPY -0.03% to 773.06, QQQ -0.31% to 720.80, DIA -0.11% to 539.02, and IWM -0.52% to 299.99. The tape is not a crash — SPY is essentially flat — but leadership flipped: last week's small-cap/tech thrust faded and the session lacked the cooperative cross-asset support that justified Friday's 7.

The dominant shock is energy. USO surged +6.74% to 125.93 from Friday's 117.98 close, a violent jump that swamps the equity move and reintroduces inflation / supply-shock risk into the regime. Gold followed through as well: GLD +1.02% to 402.53, extending the precious-metals bid that already complicated last week's Risk-on call.

Duration did not cooperate. TLT fell -0.86% to 82.05, implying a backup in long yields and removing the yield-easing tailwind from Friday. With oil ripping, gold still bid, bonds selling, and equities soft with small-cap lag, risk appetite compresses hard from 7 to 4. The only reason this is Neutral rather than Risk-off is the equity complex's relative resilience — SPY barely red — which argues the market is pricing an energy shock, not a systemic de-risking cascade.

What Supports Risk

  • SPY held nearly flat at 773.06 (-0.03%) — no equity capitulation despite the oil spike
  • No evidence of a broad liquidation cascade; declines were orderly and concentrated in higher-beta sleeves (QQQ/IWM)
  • Prior week's Risk-on momentum means some residual bid may still absorb dips if oil stabilizes

What Holds It Back

  • USO +6.74% to 125.93 is a regime-level energy shock and the clearest single risk-off impulse on the board
  • TLT -0.86% to 82.05 ends the cooperative yield backdrop that supported Friday's Risk-on 7
  • GLD +1.02% to 402.53 keeps the inflation/hedge bid alive
  • IWM -0.52% and QQQ -0.31% show risk appetite narrowing under the surface even while SPY looks calm

What Changes My Mind

  • Oil mean-reversion (USO giving back a large share of today's spike) with equities holding or reclaiming highs would reopen a path back toward 6+
  • A fresh bond rally (TLT reclaiming Friday levels) alongside stable oil would restore cross-asset Risk-on coherence
  • Conversely, another up-leg in oil with SPY/IWM breaking lower would justify dropping into Risk-off 2-3
