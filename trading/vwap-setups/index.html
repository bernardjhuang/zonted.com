How this works

A long trigger fires when the daily close moves from anywhere else to above both the earnings-anchored VWAP and YTD VWAP. A short trigger is the mirror below both. The trigger day is day 1; the ticker remains active through day 3 even if it moves back between or through the VWAPs. A fast reversal can briefly appear on both lists. Missing earnings VWAP means no signal. This is a mechanical screen, not a recommendation.

How sector-qualified setups work

Sector strength is the 50-session z-score of the sector ETF. Spread Z compares each stock with SPY; Dist Z measures distance from YTD VWAP. ENTER needs a hot sector, relative strength, and price above earnings VWAP; the + adds persistence above YTD VWAP. SHORT mirrors that setup in a weak sector. Earnings within about 9 days are flagged in the full universe. This is a mechanical screen, not a recommendation.
