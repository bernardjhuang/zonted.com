ABT setup

Unfolded · active thesis

Abbott is a defensive healthcare compounder trading near 18.2× the midpoint of raised 2026 adjusted EPS guidance. Libre is the base engine; Exact Sciences execution is the swing factor that determines whether the growth upgrade earns a better multiple.

Core thesis

Diversified Devices, Diagnostics, Nutrition, and Established Pharma cash flows support mid-to-high single-digit growth, dividends, and buybacks. The 2025–2026 de-rating created a cleaner entry than the elevated multiples of prior years.

Libre engine

FreeStyle Libre still has runway in Type 2 diabetes, international adoption, and broader metabolic monitoring. Libre Assist adds a data-and-software layer by using meal photos and AI to predict glucose impact before eating.

Exact Sciences swing factor

Abbott completed the acquisition on March 23. Exact is expected to add roughly $3B of 2026 sales, led by Cologuard and precision-oncology products. The deal is expected to dilute adjusted EPS by about $0.20 in 2026 and $0.16 in 2027 before turning accretive in 2028.

Valuation & base case

At $100.79 against raised adjusted EPS guidance of $5.45–$5.60, ABT trades at roughly 18.0–18.5× forward earnings. The working base case is 6.5–7.5% comparable sales growth, gradual margin recovery, and capital returns—enough for mid-teens annualized upside over 2–3 years if execution holds.

Benefits to scan daily

  • Cologuard volume, oncology launches, or Exact synergy delivery beats expectations.
  • Libre Type 2 and international adoption stays strong; Libre Assist improves engagement.
  • Dual glucose/ketone sensor advances through launch and reimbursement milestones.
  • Diagnostics recovery, screening utilization, or defensive healthcare flows accelerate.

Threats to scan daily

  • Exact growth, integration, or synergy shortfalls; this is the biggest thesis breaker.
  • Libre share loss, reimbursement pressure, slower Type 2 uptake, or stronger Dexcom competition.
  • New FDA, recall, or manufacturing-quality escalation in Diabetes Care.
  • Organic growth below guidance, Nutrition weakness, hospital-budget pressure, or further medtech multiple compression.

Unfolded · active research

Next catalyst

Facility edge

Hims acquired the California peptide facility in February 2025. Management said it was “up and running and operating” while onshoring R&D. That removes much of the physical-readiness lag if regulation turns favorable.

Position plan

Threats to scan daily

  • Hims fails to turn its operating facility into compliant commercial supply.

1. US-Iran War: 13 Straight Nights of Airstrikes — Hormuz Tail Risk Is Not Priced In

⚠️ ?

What happened: The US has conducted 13 consecutive nights of airstrikes against Iranian military targets. CENTCOM is actively engaging, and President Trump said Iran "wants to reach an agreement but is not yet ready" — signaling no near-term de-escalation. Iran's FM called the strikes "mindless aggression." Brent crude briefly exceeded $100/barrel before dipping back below on hopes of a deal. This is a live, shooting war between the US and a major oil producer that controls the Strait of Hormuz (through which ~20% of global oil flows).

Why it matters: If Iran closes or chokes the Strait of Hormuz, ~20 million barrels/day of oil supply is at risk. JPMorgan estimates every additional month of conflict disruption adds $7–8/barrel to Brent. Oil is already up massively — the Iran/Houthi risk premium is estimated at +$49/barrel. But the market is treating this as temporary because Brent dipped below $100 today. If Hormuz is actually blocked (not just threatened), oil goes to $130-150+ overnight, which cascades into inflation, consumer spending, and forces the Fed's hand. Houthi attacks on shipping are already choking off supply lanes.

⚠️ ?

This is what's driving the rate-hike fears above — the Fed can't ignore tariff-driven inflation. Earnings season is the first real test: watch for companies cutting guidance citing tariffs.
