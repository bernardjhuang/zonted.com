How this works

How this works

13F filings show quarter-over-quarter changes in long US holdings and arrive up to 45 days after quarter end. Flows estimate share-count changes using quarter-end prices. They exclude shorts, hide timing within the quarter, and may omit confidential positions. This is a backward-looking allocation map, not a live signal.

How this works

YTD VWAP estimates the average cost basis of shares traded this year. Above it, the average buyer is in profit; below it, the average buyer is underwater. Solid line is close, dashed line is VWAP, and triangles mark crosses. Sector and country charts show the same smoothed 50-session Z-score beneath price.
