ABT setup

Unfolded · active thesis

Abbott is a defensive healthcare compounder trading near 18.2× the midpoint of raised 2026 adjusted EPS guidance. Libre is the base engine; Exact Sciences execution is the swing factor that determines whether the growth upgrade earns a better multiple.

Core thesis

Diversified Devices, Diagnostics, Nutrition, and Established Pharma cash flows support mid-to-high single-digit growth, dividends, and buybacks. The 2025–2026 de-rating created a cleaner entry than the elevated multiples of prior years.

Libre engine

FreeStyle Libre still has runway in Type 2 diabetes, international adoption, and broader metabolic monitoring. Libre Assist adds a data-and-software layer by using meal photos and AI to predict glucose impact before eating.

Exact Sciences swing factor

Abbott completed the acquisition on March 23. Exact is expected to add roughly $3B of 2026 sales, led by Cologuard and precision-oncology products. The deal is expected to dilute adjusted EPS by about $0.20 in 2026 and $0.16 in 2027 before turning accretive in 2028.

Valuation & base case

At $100.79 against raised adjusted EPS guidance of $5.45–$5.60, ABT trades at roughly 18.0–18.5× forward earnings. The working base case is 6.5–7.5% comparable sales growth, gradual margin recovery, and capital returns—enough for mid-teens annualized upside over 2–3 years if execution holds.

Benefits to scan daily

  • Cologuard volume, oncology launches, or Exact synergy delivery beats expectations.
  • Libre Type 2 and international adoption stays strong; Libre Assist improves engagement.
  • Dual glucose/ketone sensor advances through launch and reimbursement milestones.
  • Diagnostics recovery, screening utilization, or defensive healthcare flows accelerate.

Threats to scan daily

  • Exact growth, integration, or synergy shortfalls; this is the biggest thesis breaker.
  • Libre share loss, reimbursement pressure, slower Type 2 uptake, or stronger Dexcom competition.
  • New FDA, recall, or manufacturing-quality escalation in Diabetes Care.
  • Organic growth below guidance, Nutrition weakness, hospital-budget pressure, or further medtech multiple compression.

EV recovery engine

BYD executives have confirmed humanoid development and discussed dealer distribution, upgrading robotics from pure rumor to an option. The gap is still large: no disclosed product roadmap, unit economics, production target, or material capital allocation.

Catalyst map & positioning

Next hard check: July production and sales volume, expected in early August based on BYD’s monthly disclosure cadence; no fixed company date is posted. A sustained China sales and margin recovery, concrete trade-framework progress, overseas factory wins, or a funded humanoid launch would raise conviction. Without robotics follow-through, treat this as a moderate-upside EV recovery and geo-de-risking trade—not a robotics pure play.

NTDOY setup

Cautiously constructive · accumulate on weakness · 12–24 months

Nintendo is a quality compounder facing a real but cyclical hardware-margin squeeze. Switch 2 demand was not mediocre—it reached 19.86 million units by March—but Nintendo now has to prove that elite IP, software attach, and pricing can offset a lower year-two hardware forecast and rising memory costs.

Current setup & next catalyst

At $10.68 on July 24, NTDOY trades near 17.9× trailing earnings and just above its $10.18 52-week low. Next hard test: three-month earnings on August 6, 2026. The report should show early demand ahead of the September U.S. price increase and update component-cost, hardware-margin, and software guidance.

Franchise & software engine

Mario, Zelda, Pokémon, Animal Crossing, and Nintendo’s broader character library are durable demand engines with better economics than hardware. The long case is not endless console acceleration; it is converting a large installed base into years of first-party software, digital sales, subscriptions, licensing, and repeat engagement.

Launch strength, year-two digestion

Nintendo sold 19.86M Switch 2 units through March 2026, a strong first fiscal year. It forecasts 16.50M units for the year ending March 2027, down 16.9%, while Switch 2 software is forecast to rise from 48.71M to 60.00M units. That mix shift toward software is the thesis; weaker hardware alone is not a failure.

Memory costs & price elasticity

Nintendo says rising component prices, including memory, will affect the current fiscal year and informed product price revisions. The U.S. Switch 2 MSRP rises from $449.99 to $499.99 on September 1. Gross margin already fell to 39.3% in the launch year because lower-margin Switch 2 hardware dominated the mix. The key question is whether price and software profits outrun volume sensitivity.

Benefits to scan daily

  • Memory and other component costs peak or fall faster than Nintendo assumes.
  • Switch 2 sell-through holds above guidance despite the September price increase.
  • Major first-party releases drive software units, attach rates, digital mix, and engagement.
  • A weaker yen lifts translation of overseas revenue, while affordable at-home entertainment gains share of leisure spending.

Threats to scan daily

  • DRAM, NAND, tariffs, or other input costs keep hardware profitability compressed.
  • Consumers reject the $499.99 U.S. price and year-two hardware misses Nintendo’s forecast.
  • The first-party software cadence weakens or attach rates fail to offset slower console sales.
  • Yen reversal, Japan/ADR risk-off sentiment, or valuation compression overwhelms operating progress.

Sources: Nintendo IR calendar · FY2026 financial-results material · official hardware and software unit sales · June 2026 shareholder Q&A on memory costs · U.S. Switch 2 price revision. RBLX setup

Speculative long · constructive on weakness · 18–36 months

Roblox is a high-upside cultural-platform bet, not a proven high-margin compounder yet. The asymmetric case works if a massive youth audience ages into higher-spending adults while creators, advertising, and AI-assisted discovery deepen the economy faster than safety, infrastructure, and dilution consume the upside.

Current setup & next catalyst

At $48.44 on July 24, RBLX carries a roughly $34.7B market capitalization, remains GAAP loss-making, and trades far below its $150.59 52-week high. Next hard test: Q2 earnings on July 30, 2026, after the close. Guidance and cohort quality matter more than a one-quarter headline beat.

Scale & cultural embedding

Q1 average DAUs rose 35% to 132M and hours engaged rose 43% to 31B. The moat is the combination of social graphs, identity, creator inventory, and habits—not any single game. A weaker labor market and expensive offline leisure may support engagement, but those are secondary tailwinds.

The age-up monetization test

Users over 18 represented 26% of age-checked DAUs in Q1. In the U.S., over-18 DAUs and hours both grew 40%; ages 18–34 grew more than 50% and monetized over 50% better than under-18 users. Retaining that cohort is the highest-value proof point for the multi-generational thesis.

UGC economics & profitability

Q1 revenue grew 39% to $1.4B, bookings grew 43% to $1.7B, and free cash flow reached $596M, but GAAP net loss was $246M. Creator payouts rose 50% to $423M and infrastructure plus Trust & Safety expense rose 47% to $197M. UGC externalizes content creation; it does not make the whole platform cheap to run.

Benefits to scan daily

  • 18+ DAUs, hours, and bookings grow faster than the platform while monetization stays materially higher.
  • Bookings and revenue re-accelerate without a matching surge in infrastructure or safety costs.
  • Advertising, subscriptions, discovery, and AI creation tools become measurable revenue or creator-retention drivers.
  • Creator payouts and top-developer retention strengthen the UGC flywheel without crushing platform economics.

Threats to scan daily

  • DAUs, hours, bookings, or older-user retention miss guidance or decelerate sharply.
  • A high-profile safety failure, age-verification friction, litigation, or restrictive regulation damages trust and growth.
  • Fortnite, Minecraft, short-form video, or AI-native entertainment takes younger users’ time.
  • Losses, stock compensation, creator payouts, and infrastructure spending prevent durable per-share economics.
